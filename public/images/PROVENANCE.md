# Public homepage medicine imagery

`home-tablets.png`, `home-bottles.png` and `home-care.png` were generated for the requested SmartMed POS homepage gallery. They are generic, unbranded, photorealistic illustrations, displayed with an **AI-generated illustration** label. They do not depict verified branded products, dosages, live inventory, availability or medicine prices. No pharmacy/customer/prescription data was used.

Source generation originals are retained outside the checkout at `/workspace/generated_images`. These copies are self-hosted in the application; no external image service is contacted by visitors.

Replace these files and their descriptive entries in `src/data/homeMedicineGallery.ts` with approved/licensed public assets when available. Do not publish private pharmacy product uploads without a separate explicit publication workflow.
